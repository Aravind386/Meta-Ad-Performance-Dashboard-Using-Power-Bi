
# 📊 Meta Ad Performance Dashboard

### Digital Marketing Analytics | Campaign Performance | Audience Insights

An interactive dashboard designed to analyse Meta advertising performance across Facebook and Instagram. The dashboard tracks impressions, clicks, engagement, purchases, conversion metrics, and budget-related KPIs to help evaluate campaign performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Meta Ad Performance Dashboard provides a consolidated view of advertising performance through key metrics, audience demographics, geographical distribution, weekly and hourly trends, and ad-type comparisons.

It helps explore how campaigns perform across different audience segments, locations, and advertising formats.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor advertising performance using key performance indicators.
- Analyse impressions and clicks across audience segments.
- Compare campaign performance by country, gender, and age.
- Evaluate engagement and conversion metrics.
- Compare performance across Carousel, Image, Stories, and Video ads.
- Explore weekly and hourly advertising trends.
- Support data-driven digital marketing decisions.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📈 Campaign Performance Analysis
- Track impressions, clicks, purchases, and engagements.
- Monitor CTR, engagement rate, and conversion rate.
- Review overall advertising performance through KPI cards.

### 👥 Audience Demographics
- Analyse impressions by gender.
- Compare advertising reach across age groups.
- Identify audience segments with higher impression volumes.

### 🌍 Geographical Analysis
- Compare impressions across countries.
- Review advertising reach in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, India, and other locations shown in the dashboard.

### 📅 Time-Based Analysis
- Analyse impressions by week number.
- Explore hourly impression trends.
- Identify changes in advertising activity over time.

### 📱 Ad-Type Performance
Compare performance across four advertising formats:

- Carousel
- Image
- Stories
- Video

Metrics include impressions, clicks, CTR, purchase rate, engagement rate, and conversion rate.

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters
- Dynamic metric selection
- Campaign name selection
- Target interest selection
- Facebook and Instagram platform selection

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Advertising reach:** The dashboard reports approximately 215.97K impressions.
- **Click performance:** Approximately 25.39K clicks are displayed, with a reported CTR of 11.76%.
- **Audience analysis:** Gender and age visualizations help compare impression distribution across audience segments.
- **Geographical reach:** The country comparison highlights differences in impressions across markets.
- **Ad-format comparison:** Carousel, Image, Stories, and Video formats can be compared using engagement and conversion-related metrics.
